Absolutely. You will enter the job market with a portfolio of real AI projects and a skill set most graduates — including engineering grads — do not have. That is a significant edge from Day 1 of your career.

Yes — and this is exactly who this programme is built for. All sessions are on weekends only. No leave required, no weekday commitments. Many participants finish without their employer even knowing they were upskilling.

Sessions are recorded and accessible within 24 hours. You also get written notes, the project brief, and access to mentor office hours so you never fall behind.
