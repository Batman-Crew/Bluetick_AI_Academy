Eligibility
Do I need a technical background to do this programme?+

No. You need enough depth to work effectively with engineers — not to replace them. This programme is calibrated for exactly that line. You will read and adapt code using AI tools like Cursor and Claude Code. You will build prototypes. But you will not write complex software from scratch.

How is this different from a regular product management course?+

A regular PM course teaches frameworks. This programme teaches you to build. You will come out with a working AI prototype, a structured eval framework, and a real AI product PRD — all built by you, not just studied. That portfolio is what separates you in every AI PM interview.

I am already a senior PM. Is this relevant for me?+

Especially relevant. The deeper your PM experience, the more immediately you can apply the AI depth this programme gives you. Senior PMs who complete this typically get faster access to AI roadmap ownership and leadership roles.

Can I apply this in my current PM role immediately?+

Apply it immediately. The moment your organisation has any AI feature on the roadmap, the depth you gain here changes how you contribute to every discussion — from scoping to launch to post-launch evaluation.

The Programme
What is the weekly time commitment?+

Weekends only — Saturday and Sunday, 4 hours each day. About 8 hours a week. No weekday commitments, no leave required.

Is it online or offline?+

Both. Attend in-person at our Bangalore centre, or join live online from anywhere. All sessions are recorded and available within 24 hours.

What if I miss a session?+

Sessions are recorded and accessible within 24 hours. You also get written notes, the project brief, and access to mentor office hours so you never fall behind.

The programme is 16 weekends. That is quite long. Is it worth it?+

AI PM is not a one-week skilling exercise. The foundation modules (M1–M9) give you the technical depth to work with engineers. The PM specialisation modules (S1–S6) give you the product frameworks specific to AI. Together, they give you something most PMs do not have — and that is exactly why the market pays a premium for it.
